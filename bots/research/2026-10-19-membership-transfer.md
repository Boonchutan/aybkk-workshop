# Membership transfer: 30-70% window or not? (research 19 Oct 2026)

Boonchu asked: transferable (only between 30% and 70% of the term used, not 1-month; 10-class pack while 3+ left) or non-transferable? Why? What is the standard? What does Reddit say?
Method: 4 researchers (members' voice, industry, Thai law, design), 4 skeptics, 1 critic. Reddit read through an archive (reddit.com blocked to tools); Pantip opening posts only.

## Verdict
Do NOT use the 30-70% window. Recommended: non-transferable by default (the Mysore standard) plus ONE written hardship transfer for 3/6/12-month memberships: moving away or doctor says stop, freeze does not fit, once only, to someone new to AYBKK, same end date, flat fee (suggested 1,500 THB = one drop-in; Boonchu decides), ask on LINE first. 10-class pack: whole balance once while 3+ left, same expiry.

Why not the window: (1) wrong problem: a friend trying 1-2 classes is LENDING, already banned; the teacher who knows every face stops it (forums say staff who know you are the main deterrent); (2) a no-reason transfer creates resale that undercuts our own passes (about 6 months left on a 12-month = about 27,600 THB vs 32,000 new 6-month; about 3 months left on a 6-month = about 16,000 vs 22,000 new 3-month); (3) staff must count days per pass and any doubt is read for the member (Unfair Contract Terms Act s.4 para 2); (4) the 30% floor removes the reassurance a 6-month buyer wants.

## Standard
- Mysore/Ashtanga shalas: non-transferable and non-refundable everywhere found (The Shala NYC "No refunds, extensions, transfers, or freezes"; Mission Ashtanga SF; Mysore Cincinnati; Innocere; Astanga Yoga London). SABKK Bangkok: holds with evidence, nothing on transfer. No studio uses a "% of term used" window.
- Boutique studios that allow transfer: reason + discretion + fee (BASE Bangkok: relocation or long injury, 25% fee), whole package with same expiry, or family only.
- Gyms: "non-transferable" on paper but managed transfers (Fitness First TH: management approval; receiver new, fee, same term).

## Members (Reddit/Pantip)
- They want an exit valve when life happens (injury, pregnancy, moving), mostly a pause, not cash.
- A clearly written "no refund" is accepted by about a third; called a rip-off when the studio changes what it delivers or refuses a documented medical/pregnancy pause.
- Wherever transfer is open, resale appears (Pantip listings for Fitness First, WE, Jatomi, Yoga & Me; HardwareZone; r/yoga). People game exceptions ("say you're pregnant").
- 0 of 33 r/Bangkok yoga posts about transfers; no Mysore shala transfer story found.

## Thai law (have a Thai lawyer check before printing)
- 2011 fitness contract rule (Royal Gazette 22 Jun 2011, https://www.ocpb.go.th/download/law/1884622.pdf) EXCLUDES businesses offering only yoga (ข้อ 2). So AYBKK is not forced to allow transfer (its ข้อ 3(8)), doctor's-note refunds, or a max 1-year term. RISK: if Pilates, runners or office-syndrome classes are bundled into the membership, AYBKK may fall under it (free transfer on notice, refunds, 1-year cap that breaks the 13-month pass). Keep membership yoga-only; sell other classes as separate packs.
- Non-transferable terms are lawful (CCC s.303, by agreement).
- "No refunds" with no exception can be cut down (Unfair Contract Terms Act B.E. 2540 s.4). OCPB direction: beauty/spa 15 Sep 2025 lists transfer bans and refund refusal as unfair; swim school ordered to refund unused lessons after a branch closed (Pattaya Mail 12 Aug 2026). Safer wording: non-refundable after purchase, except if AYBKK cannot provide the classes paid for (close or move) or where Thai law requires.
- Price display: กกร. 73/2569 (1 Jul 2026, https://www.dit.go.th/media/cejpahs1/20260707163750_4880.pdf), exercise venues listed: show VAT next to EVERY price (or VAT-inclusive prices), Thai wording next to prices, never charge more than shown. Poster now shows "+VAT" by each price.
- "Free month" claims need their conditions stated (OCPB ad guideline): 12-month tag reverted to "you get 13" / "ใช้ได้ 13 เดือน". 21 Oct: Boonchu dropped the 13th month (12 months, same 59,900), so no free claim is left on the poster.
- Condition text at least 1/4 of main text size (min 2 mm) on print.
