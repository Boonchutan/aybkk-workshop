In-Depth Call Questions

20 questions for the call with a serious lead for In-Depth Mysore, in call order. Ask one question at a time, then stay quiet and let them talk. You will not use all 20: pick the ones that fit this person. A kind "not yet" is a good result too.

Step 1 Learn their practice

Kit step 2: Their story

Learn how they practice and why they applied, before you talk about the program.

  1. 01

    How do you practice now: in a Mysore room, in led classes, or alone? How many days a week?

    你现在怎么练？在 Mysore 练习室、上引导课，还是自己一个人练？一周练几天？

    What to listen for

    Primary Series by heart, plus Mysore and led class? That is the entry level.

    Added: entry check

  2. 02

    What has kept you practicing all these years, when many people stop?

    很多人练着练着就停了。这些年是什么让你一直练下来？

    What to listen for

    Habit, a teacher, or only new asana? This shows what keeps them going.

    Original #64

  3. 03

    What changed recently in your practice or teaching that made you apply?

    最近你的练习或教学有什么变化，让你决定来申请？

    What to listen for

    A real event, like a stuck practice or students who stop coming.

    Original #62

  4. 04

    What is the hardest thing in your practice or your teaching right now?

    现在你的练习或教学里，最难的是什么？

    What to listen for

    One clear, real problem, or only a wish to learn more asana?

    Original #1

Step 2 Find the real problem

Kit step 2: Their story

Get real stories and details, so you see if this program can help.

  1. 05

    Can you tell me about one real time this problem happened, in practice or class?

    能跟我讲一次具体的经历吗？就是这个问题真的出现的那次，在练习里或课上都可以。

    What to listen for

    A true story with details, not a general idea.

    Original #14

  2. 06

    If you teach: what happens in your classes that you wish did not happen?

    如果你在教课：你的课上会出现什么情况，是你希望不要出现的？

    What to listen for

    Students who stop coming back? Write down their exact words.

    Original #6 · teachers

  3. 07

    What have you already tried: workshops, trips to Mysore, other teachers, changes in your classes?

    你已经试过哪些办法？工作坊、去 Mysore、跟别的老师学，还是调整自己的课？

    What to listen for

    What failed and why. Is this program truly different from what they tried?

    Original #3

  4. 08

    Of all you told me, which part is hardest to change on your own?

    你跟我说的这些里，哪一部分最难靠自己改变？

    What to listen for

    We can be their teacher for 30 days. We cannot give them time.

    Original #8

Step 3 If nothing changes

Kit step 2: Their story

Let them name what is not working now. Ask gently, never push on pain.

  1. 09

    If your daily practice stays just as it is, what will that mean for you?

    如果你每天的练习一直就是现在这样，这对你意味着什么？

    What to listen for

    Real worry, or real peace. If peace, they may not need this.

    Original #31

  2. 10

    If you teach: if you teach the same way for years, what happens to your students?

    如果你在教课：如果你很多年都用同样的方式教，你的学生会怎么样？

    What to listen for

    Do students stay or leave? If most stay, the need may be small.

    Original #38 · teachers

  3. 11

    What would make this problem important enough that you want to change it?

    要发生什么，你才会觉得这个问题重要到想去改变它？

    What to listen for

    If nothing would, this may not be the time. Do not push.

    Original #40

Step 4 The practice they want

Kit step 2: Their story

Hear the practice they want, and check if 30 days can serve it.

  1. 12

    What kind of practitioner, or teacher, do you want to become?

    你想成为什么样的练习者，或者什么样的老师？

    What to listen for

    Deep practice over years, or a title? This program gives no authorization.

    Original #71

  2. 13

    If you joined, how would you know a year later that it really helped?

    如果你来参加了，一年以后你怎么知道它真的帮到了你？

    What to listen for

    Real signs, like a steady daily practice, not a new asana or title.

    Original #48

Step 5 Fit and worries

Kit step 6: Questions and worries

Find real reasons to say no: life, body, time, or a better choice.

  1. 14

    What would need to change so you can be away from home and work for 30 days?

    需要有哪些改变，你才能离开家、离开工作 30 天？

    What to listen for

    A real plan, or a sign this is not the right time.

    Original #57

  2. 15

    What worries you about joining: an injury, the way we teach, or something else?

    来参加的话，你担心什么？是伤的问题、我们的教法，还是别的？

    What to listen for

    Injury or the method. Answer with facts, and promise nothing about the body.

    Original #89

  3. 16

    What other teachers, trips, or courses are you thinking about for your practice?

    为了你的练习，你还在考虑哪些别的老师、行程或课程？

    What to listen for

    Other good choices. If another teacher fits them better, say so.

    Original #85

  4. 17

    What would make 30 days of Mysore with us the wrong step for you?

    在什么情况下，跟我们练 30 天 Mysore 对你来说不合适？

    What to listen for

    Real reasons to say no. If one is strong, let them go kindly.

    Original #84

Step 6 The honest end

Kit step 7: Yes or no

Give your honest view and let them choose yes, no, or wait.

  1. 18

    We watched your practice video. Would you like our honest view, even if it is 'not yet'?

    我们看了你的练习视频。你想听我们真实的看法吗？就算是「还没到时候」。

    What to listen for

    Are they open to 'not yet'? Then give the real view, kindly.

    Original #97

  2. 19

    What do you still need to know or do before you can decide?

    在做决定之前，你还需要了解什么，或者做些什么？

    What to listen for

    The real reason they wait, and a reply date they choose themselves.

    Original #99

  3. 20

    Which is right for you: joining this group, waiting, or not joining?

    对你来说，哪个更合适：参加这一期、再等等，还是不参加？

    What to listen for

    Yes, no and wait are equal. If yes, explain the payment steps.

    Original #100

Never say on this call

  • "Only today", "last seats", or any deadline that is not real.
  • Calling this program teacher training, authorization or certification. It gives a certificate of completion, not authorization.
  • Any promise of results: a new asana, more students, "your students will stay", or a healed injury.
  • "Investment", "it pays for itself", or "you can't afford to miss this".
  • Anything that makes Boonchu's authorization bigger than it is.
  • "Let me know soon", or contacting them again and again after the call.
