# What to watch first, and what to notice

Every link below was checked to exist in September 2026. Netflix titles are worldwide. "Free" means no subscription needed.

## Start here, in this order

1. **Golden Globes 2020 opening monologue** (YouTube, free, 8 min): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S7OOJWtk550 — read along with the transcript: https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/general-news/transcript-ricky-gervais-golden-globes-2020-opening-monologue-1266516/ . Notice the first line: he opens on himself, not a star. That line buys permission for every joke after it.
2. **The Office (UK), "David Brent's Motivational Speech Disaster"** (his own channel, free): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HIrhXek1MQQ . Count the seconds of dead air after a bad joke. Nobody signals the joke. The silence is the joke.
3. **Humanity** (Netflix, 2018). The nut-allergy story in the middle third: read once, flat, then returned to until the end. The model callback. Trailer: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XAwU3Lyn--U . Free transcript: https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/comedy/ricky-gervais-humanity-transcript/
4. **After Life, Season 1 Episode 1** (Netflix, 2019): https://www.netflix.com/title/80998491 . The sad true thing is planted in the first minute, before any joke, so every cruel line after reads as pain. Season 2 Episode 6, Anne on the bench: "Happiness is amazing. It's so amazing, it doesn't matter if it's yours or not."
5. **The Ricky Gervais Show podcast, first episode** (Apple Podcasts, free): https://podcasts.apple.com/th/podcast/the-ricky-gervais-show/id1774762065 . One plain question, then he stays quiet and lets Karl be the joke.

## The moves, one clip each

- **Flat face:** Noah's Ark reading from the Science show (free clip): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=764Qiu75K1Y
- **Target bigger than the room:** Golden Globes 2011, the Charlie Sheen list that ends "And that was a Monday" (free): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BvHXzP2SpLA
- **True thing after the joke:** the Mel Gibson introduction, Golden Globes 2016 (free): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_Ty-z_3M1Z0 . "I still feel bad about it" comes before the knife.
- **Callback:** Learn Guitar with David Brent, any episode (free playlist): https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLPf6bUqN1Brt7YUifE52IEJV4l553NZ1v
- **Let the other thing be the joke:** taking his Emmy back from Steve Carell, 2008 (free): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RZp6cR4bxbY . Gervais does all the talking; Carell never smiles.

## Him explaining himself

- "The Principles of Comedy", Big Think (free, 4 min): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WTl_xjOyZsc
- "The Difference Between American and British Humour", TIME essay (free): https://time.com/3720218/difference-between-american-british-humour/
- Oxford Union full Q&A (free): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xwED4v5G3HA
- Colbert, "Go Head-To-Head On Religion" (free): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P5ZOwNK6n9U — watch how he stays polite and starts with "I don't know". Do not copy the subject.
- SuperNature (Netflix, 2022) opens with him teaching the room what irony is. The most direct lesson he has given about how his jokes work.
