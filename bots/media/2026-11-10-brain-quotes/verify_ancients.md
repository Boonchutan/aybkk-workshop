## Slide cuts after checking (all exact)

1. in education practice must be used before theory, and the body be trained before the mind
2. the care of the body ought to precede that of the soul ... and our care of the body for the sake of the soul.
3. Let us assume, then, as a first principle in relation both to the body and soul of very young creatures, that nursing and moving about by day and night is good for them all (second line, p. 171: "the use of exercise and motion in the earliest years of life greatly contributes to create a part of virtue in the soul.")
4. we should not move the body without the soul or the soul without the body
5. the two arts of music and gymnastic ... the teachers of both have in view chiefly the improvement of the soul.
6. even in the process of thinking, in which the use of the body seems to be reduced to a minimum, ... grave mistakes may often be traced to bad health.
7. (rejected)
8. When exercise can render service both to body and mind, each in its own special form of excellence, it is a blessing indeed.
9. Methinks that the moment my legs begin to move, my thoughts begin to flow
10. Walking has the best value as gymnastics for the mind.
