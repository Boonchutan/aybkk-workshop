## Safety (the business runs in Thailand, China and Russia)
- The Ramayana is holy to many people, and Thailand's own version (Ramakien) is tied to the Thai kings (the kings carry the name Rama).
  So: always respectful. Never a joke about Rama, Sita, Lakshmana, Hanuman or any god or sage. Use the Indian names. Never mention Thai kings,
  never write "King Rama", never compare with Thai royalty. Do not mix in Ramakien scenes.
- Thrones and crowns are never the point of an episode: tell the exile as a father's promise and a son's choice, not as a fight for a throne.
- No war. No killing scene shown, no blood. A death that the story needs (e.g. Jatayu) is told in one gentle line, bedtime level.
- Nothing a 5-year-old should not hear: the cutting of Surpanakha's nose and ears, Ahalya's adultery, Ravana's threats to Sita are OUT or told in one safe line.
- No politics of today (the film or the story used by any party), no religion against religion.
