# The Feifei demo — under 2 minutes, exact clicks

Before the call, once:
1. Open `cn.aybkk.net/loc-seed.html`, enter the admin key, press **Seed now**.
   Copy the three passcodes it shows — they appear once, never again.
   (Terminal alternative: `DATABASE_URL=... node scripts/seed-chengdu.js`.)
2. As owner, open `/loc-admin.html`, press **Statement draft** for this month,
   check it with **View**, then **Issue**. The demo needs one issued statement.
3. On your phone AND a second device (or a second browser profile), sign in:
   phone = Feifei's passcode on `/partner.html`, laptop = your owner passcode on
   `/loc-admin.html`. Stay signed in so the call has zero typing.

Practise the run once. The whole point is that it looks effortless.

---

## The run (share your phone screen first)

**0:00 — Her view.** Open `/partner.html`, already signed in as Feifei.
Say: *"This is your page. Your passcode, not mine."*
Point, top to bottom, no clicking yet:
- 本月收入 — everything the location took this month
- 你的分成 — her share, with the percentage written on it
- 你已代收 — what landed in HER WeChat/Alipay
- 月底结算 — the one line that matters: who transfers what on the 1st

**0:30 — The ledger.** Tap the latest statement → **查看 View**.
Say: *"Every payment. Who paid, how, into whose account. Refunds are separate
lines — nothing is ever deleted or edited in this system."*
Scroll to the bottom: the totals, and the net-transfer line in the pink box.

**0:50 — Her voice.** Scroll to 留言 comments. Type one live:
「测试：这个数字我要核对」 → **留言**.
Say: *"If a number looks wrong to you, you write it here — on the record, both
of us see it, it cannot be quietly removed."*

**1:05 — She can take it away.** Tap **下载 CSV** — it opens in Excel with
Chinese intact — and **打印/存为PDF**.
Say: *"You never have to ask me for the accounts. They are yours to download."*

**1:20 — The trust move. Switch to your laptop screen** (owner view).
In Split settings, change 70/30 to 60/40 and save.
Say: *"Watch — I just changed the deal in my favour... against myself, for the
demo."* Switch back to her phone, refresh: **this month's live numbers moved to
40%** — but open the issued statement again: **still 70/30, unchanged**.
Say: *"Issued statements are frozen with the percentages of the day. Even I
cannot rewrite history. And look —"* show 操作记录 on her dashboard: the
settings change is in the audit log, with before and after, under my name.*
Change the split back to 70/30. Do this on the call too — visibly.

**1:50 — Close.** Back on her dashboard.
Say: *"Your students, your house, your page. Everything that happens in it,
you see. That's the deal in software."*

## If she asks…

- **"Can you see my other NaSaDi business?"** — No. This shows only AYBKK
  Chengdu payments recorded in this system. Nothing touches her systems.
- **"What if I disagree with a number?"** — The comment on the statement, on
  the record. And nothing is ever edited: corrections are new lines.
- **"Who else can see this?"** — Her own staff get their own passcodes, same
  read-only view. Lou Han records payments but cannot touch the percentages.
  Only Boonchu can change the split — and every change is logged where she
  can see it.
- **"What about events / workshops?"** — Phase 2, after signing: teacher share
  first, then expenses, then the same 70/30 on the host net — the formula is
  already written and tested (the ¥200,000 example: AYBKK 49,000 / NaSaDi
  21,000 after ¥10,000 expenses).
