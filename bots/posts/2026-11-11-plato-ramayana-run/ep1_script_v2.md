Toolkit pick: Name-the-Feeling + Fork-First Story. Why: his own first line names the flat morning every long-time practitioner knows (practiced, nothing happened), and "yet" opens a loop that pays three times: day five, day six, tomorrow. The story has a natural stop at day five (no word, no monster, no sleep), so "What you do?... Sleep?" makes the viewer choose before the book answers with "be ready". That choice is the pull that carries them from 0:19 to the arrow.

Toolkit choice: Gervais deadpan + /story 4-beat (Draft D): its flat Mysore-morning joke covered the drop-off after the story best. But "Teacher walk past you... twice / Sometimes that teacher is me" sounds like a teacher who ignores students, which goes against the confirmed line and the Habit Gap. He did not ask for jokes, and it pushed out the fixed Mysore-room line. | Vibe Samurai (flat statement, ladder of five days, turn, flip): the days make a ready ladder, but its turn pulls toward the banned "not X but Y" line, and it has no stop where the viewer must choose.
