# AYBKK direction: The Habit Gap (set by Boonchu, 4 Oct 2026)

Every bot reads this before writing, advising or building. It beats older content ideas, never the hard rules.

## The one idea
People spend more on wellness every year (trackers, supplements, apps, cold plunges). Results do not improve. The problem is not tools. It is quitting.
**Mysore is a habit system: same practice, same time, a teacher who notices when you are gone.** That is what AYBKK sells. Not poses, not flexibility, not "yoga".

One line: **"Your watch knows you slept badly. It won't get you on the mat at 6. We will."**

## What people want (and how we answer)
| They want | We say |
|---|---|
| Proof (they track everything) | A 12-week check: classes done, resting heart rate, sleep. Numbers they already wear. |
| Get stronger, age well | Ashtanga builds strength with your own body weight, for decades. |
| Calm the mind (top reason to exercise) | Breath-led practice. The calm is trained, not bought. |
| Sleep | Morning practice, early nights. Students report it; we show it. |
| Together, calm, not a bar | A quiet room of people who show up. Belonging without noise. |
| Traditional, trusted | A 100-year method, taught by an Authorized teacher. (Never inflate the authorization.) |

## What we do NOT do
- No cold plunge, sauna, red light, reformer machines. Big chains (Club Pilates, GymNation) win on equipment and price. We win on habit and teacher.
- S31 Pilates, runners, office-syndrome classes are front doors only. Judge them by how many move into Mysore.
- No trend content for its own sake. Every piece must lead to: a Mysore trial, a renewal, or the 180K program.
- Never use a statistic in public until it is checked against its source.

## Per money engine
- **Shala:** sell the outcome (stronger, calmer, better sleep, aging well) + the habit (we notice when you are gone). Offer: first month with a 12-week proof check.
- **180K program (China, Russia, Bangkok):** pitch to studio owners and teachers: "How to build students who stay for years." Retention is what they pay for. Their students treat wellness like fashion; our graduates turn it into a habit.
- **Workshops by graduates in China:** same message: from trend to habit.

## Action plan

### 1. App: the profile becomes the habit system (Neo builds, in this order)
1. **"We miss you" list** for M on the staff page: students with an active pass who have not come in 7+ days. One tap sends a warm LINE message draft. (The teacher who notices you are gone, as a system.)
2. **Attendance streak** on each profile: weeks in a row with 3+ practices. Habit made visible.
3. **12-week proof check**: at week 0, 6 and 12 the student enters resting heart rate, sleep hours and one line on how they feel (from their watch or phone). The profile shows the change next to their photos.
4. **Renewal with proof**: 14 days before the pass ends, the profile shows their numbers ("41 practices, resting HR 68 to 61") and the renewal button. Offer at the moment they feel progress.
5. **Daily journal** (the CN system) linked in, after the photo test.

### 2. Offer (Hormozi)
- **Shala, new students:** "Your first 12 weeks: we check your progress at week 0, 6 and 12." Same price, more certainty, less effort deciding.
- **180K program:** "Teach students who stay for years." Show retention from AYBKK as proof (numbers checked first).

### 3. Reels (Plato, 3 a week on this)
- "Your watch knows you slept badly. It won't get you on the mat at 6. I will."
- "You don't need another app. You need someone who notices when you don't show up."
- "Everyone starts in January. Ashtanga is for February."
- "I have watched 2,000 students. The strong ones weren't the flexible ones. They were the ones who came back."
- "Cold plunge: 3 minutes. Habit: 3 years. Guess which one changes your body."

### 4. Score (Machi tracks weekly)
Students not seen in 7+ days, profiles opened, streaks, renewals due and won.

### Weak points to watch
- Trend numbers come from a chat search: check each one before public use.
- A habit system only works if M actually uses the "we miss you" list every day. Build it, then check the Progress box.
- Front-door classes (Pilates, runners) must be judged by conversion to Mysore, not by attendance.
