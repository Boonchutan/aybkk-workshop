The Endocrine Event · 内分泌事件

An endocrine event is when your body detects a stimulus —
and releases hormones to regulate internal balance.

当身体察觉到刺激，便释放激素，来调节内在的平衡。
Stress压力 Effort用力 Fasting禁食 Emotion情绪 Energy能量 Growth生长 Mood心情 Recovery恢复

Testosterone & Growth · 睾酮与生长
Intensity + volume →
a natural testosterone boost
  • Jump-backs, holds, Mysore self-pacing & led-class fatigue 跳后 · 停留 · 疲惫
  • Asana as isometric intensity → muscle recruitment → anabolic signal 合成代谢信号
Cortisol & Controlled Stress · 皮质醇与可控压力
Mysore is a controlled adrenal challenge.
迈索尔自习，是一场可控的肾上腺挑战。
Fear & challenge→ Cortisol + Testosterone
  • Mobilizes energy — glucose & fat 动员能量
  • Sharpens the response 锐化反应
  • Reduces inflammation 降低炎症
Hormesis · 适应性压力

Mysore demands personal regulation:

  • Pace yourself 自我节奏
  • Push within limits 在限度内推进
  • Recover while still in effort — Marichyasana B/D, Kapotasana 在用力中恢复
Small, repeated, controlled stress →
long-term resilience & endocrine adaptability
Dopamine & Presence · 多巴胺与临在
Dopamine rises during the process of progressing —
not at the finish.
  • Learning a new transition · a jump-through improving 进步中
  • Tracking the breath with precision 精准地跟随呼吸
  • Cold showers · staying present 冷水 · 临在
Serotonin & Mood · 血清素与情绪
Serotonin rises with repetition, rhythm & steady progress.
  • Tofu, eggs, seeds give you tryptophan — an essential amino acid your body can't make — the raw material for serotonin 色氨酸 · 必需氨基酸
  • Stable serotonin → motivation, consistency, resilience 稳定 → 坚持
Neuroscience of Habit & Discipline · 习惯的神经科学
  • The nervous system is the foundation — it builds the muscle (the mind–muscle link) 神经系统建造肌肉
  • Practice forms cortical maps of movement 皮层地图
  • Feeling the muscles, joints, heartbeat, fatigue builds insula awareness — body awareness, pain tolerance, emotional regulation, self-observation 脑岛觉知
Two Things, One Process · 一个过程的两面
Cortical mapping
is the conscious sculpting.
皮层地图，是有意识的雕琢。
Muscle memory
is the subconscious result.
肌肉记忆，是潜意识的结果。

A cortical map is the detailed model your brain builds of your body and its movement — what to move, how to move it, and how it should feel.

困难的事？一次，只做一部分。
Each part completed = a dopamine kick.

Cortisol as Fuel · 皮质醇作为燃料

Cortisol mobilizes fuel — it breaks the body's stores down.

Blood sugar 血糖 ↑ → energy Fat 脂肪 → steady fuel Protein 蛋白质 → broken down
Recovery comes when cortisol falls — the rest-and-repair phase. 恢复，发生在皮质醇下降之时。

They're part of the process.
激素不是「结果」——它们，是过程的一部分。
