# Plato · 4 Oct 2026 · Hidden Stories of Ashtanga (no Ep. number until filmed; next = Ep. 6 if Ep. 5 Foolish Old Man is posted first) · "The Tail Nobody Could Lift"

Route: Lane A · last 3 posts: Small Steps carousel (B), Learn by doing B5 (B), Ep. 4 Tittibhasana (A) · next due: Lane A is on route; Day 1 to Day 30 (Lane B) from 13 Oct.
Toolkit pick: Myth-Kill (hook) + Hero's Journey, cut to the 4 /story beats. Why: the news kills a belief ("strength is for the young"), and Bhima's walk is a small hero's test: proud, fails, bows, comes back wiser. Not Triple Hook or Fork-First (used 1, 2, 3 Oct).
Other options (ask for a rewrite): (1) Win/Loss Frame + /story: "Lose your legs, you lose your freedom" vs "one session a week keeps them". (2) Gervais deadpan opener: "I'm 53. Science says I still have 37 years to get strong. Great. No excuse now."

## The link
- Trigger: a strength review out this week (Newcastle University, The Conversation, ScienceDaily 2 Oct 2026): even one strength session a week helps; "Even people in their 80s and 90s can build or maintain muscle strength."
- Story: Bhima, the strongest man, tells an old sick monkey to move. He cannot lift the old monkey's tail with both arms. The monkey is Hanuman, his brother (Mahabharata, Vana Parva, sec. 146).
- Behavior: Bhima judged strength by age. He was wrong.
- Asana: Utkatasana ("fierce" asana). A bodyweight squat, in every Sun Salutation B and held 5 breaths at the end of the standing asanas. Strength you train every practice, at any age.

## Hooks (/hook, answered by Plato)
Topic: strength and age. Consensus: strength belongs to the young; after 60 you only lose it. Teacher's view: in the shala, strength comes from what you repeat each week, not your birth year.
1. Authority: "I am 53. Science says my strongest years can still come."
2. Contrarian: "Everybody say strength leave you at 60. It's not true."
3. Stakes: "Weak legs take your freedom first. One session a week stops it."
4. Question: "How old is too old to get stronger? Scientists just answered."
5. Pattern interrupt: "This week, scientists said: even at ninety, you can get stronger."
Plato's pick: 5. It opens with the news, has a number people can see on screen, and kills a belief everyone over 40 carries. A 10-year practitioner over 40 stops on it.

## Script (60 s, spoken to camera, his spoken English)
HOOK (0-4 s)
"This week, scientists say... even at ninety... you can get stronger."
[card: "80s and 90s can still build strength" · Newcastle Univ. 2026]

CONTEXT (4-12 s)
"One strength session a week. Already enough to start.
But most people still believe... strength is for the young.
Tonight, I tell my son a story about this."

ORDEAL (12-42 s), bedtime voice
"Bhima. The strongest man in the Mahabharata.
He walk in the forest. On the path... an old monkey, lying down.
Bhima say: 'Move.'
The monkey say: 'I am old. I am sick. I cannot get up. You move my tail.'
Bhima smile. One hand. ...The tail don't move.
Two hands. ...Don't move.
He sweat. His face all wrinkle.
[silent beat, 1 s]
The old monkey... was Hanuman. Bhima's brother."
(to the adults) "Bhima looked at the age. He never looked at the strength."

TAKEAWAY (42-55 s)
"In Ashtanga, we have Utkatasana. Fierce asana.
Bend the knees. Sit back, like a chair. Arms up. Breathe.
It's a squat. The same thing the scientists talk about.
Every practice. At twenty. At seventy. Same asana."
(Boonchu: swap these cue lines for your own Utkatasana words.)

RETURN (55-60 s)
"Ninety is not too old. ... Only stopping is too old."

Payoff: the hook says "even at ninety you can get stronger"; the last line closes it: age is not the limit, stopping is.
One ordeal: Bhima cannot lift the tail. One lesson: strength is not about age.

Check before filming:
- The study is a review article (a summary of many studies), not one new trial. Say "scientists say", not "a new study proves". Do not say Utkatasana was in the study.
- Text: Bhima wants to send the monkey to Yama (death). Left out for the bedtime voice. Hanuman says "In consequence of age, I have no strength to get up" (Ganguli). Hanuman is shown with respect, never mocked.
- Hanuman also appeared 2 Oct (Surasa story, Hanumanasana). This is a different story and a different asana. If two Hanuman stories in one week feels like too much, hold this one a week.
- Any student shown in Utkatasana needs their OK (elders especially).
