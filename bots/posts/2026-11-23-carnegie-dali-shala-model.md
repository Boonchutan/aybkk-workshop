# Dali: economy, fitness market and the Ashtanga shala model

**No. I would not open, own or license a shala in Dali, because no rented room pays in any demand case.**

Written on Mon 23 Nov 2026 for Boonchu, AYBKK. It builds on yesterday's Dali note ("Dali Yoga Fitness Ashtanga Research", 22 Nov 2026) and does not repeat it. Dali is 大理, in Yunnan, China. Not for posting: it holds the yearly goal and income sums.

## 0. The answer on one page

To license means to let someone use the name and method for a fee. A demand case is one guess at how many people would pay: low, base (the middle guess) or high.

If a shala runs there at all, it has one shape. A graduate who already lives in Dali teaches at home, on **6 mats**. The one daily Mysore room in Dali already has that form. AYBKK cannot own such a room, and I would keep the AYBKK name off it.

**The 3 biggest risks**

1. No spare demand. The town's only daily Mysore room has cut its hours and two of its three prices. A new room fills by taking that teacher's members. Nobody has asked the teacher there.
2. No legal space where the right people live. The cheap spaces are homes. A home needs the neighbours' consent for business use, and the fire step before opening cannot be done there.
3. The rules on work and on names. Visa-free entry does not cover work. A ban would end every China stop. A franchise is a deal where one business lets another trade under its name and method for a fee. A name plus a method plus a fee is treated as one. That needs 2 shops you run yourself. AYBKK has 1.

> **What the law says** (texts read on copies, 23 Nov 2026)
>
> - Business form. A foreigner may not be an individual business owner. So a room you own must be a company (FACT, the 2024 list of fields closed to foreign money, note 2) [S220][S221].
> - Yoga and fitness are not on that list of 29 items (FACT) [S220].
> - A company can have one owner. The Company Law sets no lowest capital. Promised capital is paid in within 5 years (FACT) [S222].
> - Work. A foreigner who works in China needs a work permit and a work-type residence permit. The fine is 5,000 to 20,000 CNY for the worker and 10,000 CNY a person for whoever hires. Being sent home brings a ban on entry for 1 to 5 years (FACT) [S120].
> - Visa-free entry for Thai passports is 30 days a stay and 90 days in any 180. It does not cover work (FACT, in force 1 Mar 2024) [S119].
> - A 2014 rule on short-term work lists "completing specific work for a Chinese cooperation partner" for under 90 days. Volunteers who are unpaid, or paid from abroad, are left out. This is a law firm's list. The rule's own text was not opened (VENDOR CLAIM, note of 11 Dec 2014, re-opened 23 Nov 2026) [S246].
> - Work permit, class B. The usual line is a degree, 2 years of related work and age up to 60. The guide lets offices relax these. Over 60, the employer adds a letter that covers health insurance (FACT, the Shenzhen copy of the national guide; no Dali guide was found) [S223][S224]. The permit is free. The residence permit costs 400 to 1,000 CNY (FACT, a Jinan guide) [S225].
> - A home used for business needs the consent of every affected owner in the building (FACT, Civil Code Art. 279) [S181].
> - Leases. The longest term is 20 years. Sub-letting (a tenant renting the space on to someone else) needs the owner's consent, or the owner may end the lease (FACT, Civil Code Art. 705 and 716) [S181].
> - Fire. A "public gathering place" must pass a fire safety step before it opens. Opening without it costs 30,000 to 300,000 CNY. A police rule lists "commercial fitness and leisure places". A yoga room is not named (FACT) [S227][S228]. A full fire design review starts only above 2,500 m2 (FACT) [S229].
> - Hygiene licence (a health permit for public places). It covers public places except parks, sports venues and transport. Yunnan's own list was not found (FACT) [S226].
> - The lake. Within 100 m of the water on the west and north shores, nothing may be built, rebuilt or extended unless it serves the lake. Fines run from 200,000 to 1,000,000 CNY. The next zone allows only public works (FACT, 2019 text; a later change could not be ruled out) [S179].
> - The Old Town. No new building in the core. Outdoor adverts and outside fittings must fit the town's style. Street fronts of new or rebuilt buildings near the core use old-style materials. The law has no article on shop-sign size (FACT, 2022 text) [S180].
> - Guesthouses. A prefecture law has been in force since 1 Jan 2026. It matters for any live-in house (FACT) [S182][S32].
> - Yunnan's long-stay list bans lodging trade in rented houses without legal papers (FACT, in force 20 Nov 2026) [S35].
> - A licence does not save a business in a building without papers. In May 2026 one lake town told 7 licensed businesses in such buildings to stop (FACT, a press report opened by the cost reviewer) [S185].
> - Franchise. Only a company may be the franchisor. It needs 2 shops it runs itself, each open over 1 year. It files within 15 days of the first contract. Fines are 100,000 to 500,000 CNY (FACT) [S230]. Papers made abroad are accepted if they are legalised, which means stamped so that another country accepts them (FACT, filing measures, opened by the cost reviewer on a law-database copy) [S233]. In Aug 2026 the top court upheld a 100,000 CNY fine even after the fees were paid back. It also held a deal signed by a non-company void (FACT) [S231]. A law firm says fees under any name count, "trademark licence fee" included (VENDOR CLAIM) [S232].
> - The name. A trademark is a registered name or logo. The fee to file one is 270 CNY a class online. The office took 4 months on average in 2025. A new Trademark Law starts on 1 Jan 2027 (FACT) [S234][S235][S236]. The cost reviewer adds that China gives a trademark to the first to file. No law text was opened for that line.
> - Tax breaks to 31 Dec 2027. No VAT while sales stay under 100,000 CNY a month; a month that reaches the line is taxed in full, at 1%. Income tax of an individual business is halved. A small company pays 5% of profit (FACT) [S237][S238][S239].
> - Money sent abroad. A fee or a dividend (profit a company pays its owner) paid to a foreign company loses 10% at the source. The Chinese buyer also holds back 6% VAT on a licence fee (FACT) [S241][S242][S239].
> - Tax treaty (a deal between two countries on who taxes what). If your people give services in China for more than 183 days in any 12 months, China may tax the Thai side as if it had a base there (FACT, 1986 treaty, read on a copy) [S243].
> - Prepaid passes. A buyer may ask for the money back within 7 days. A buyer may cancel if an "unlimited" pass is not served as normal. The rule names fitness and training (FACT, top court rule in force 1 May 2025, Art. 1, 13 and 14, re-opened on a copy on 23 Nov 2026) [S108][S247]. Shanghai caps prepaid passes.
