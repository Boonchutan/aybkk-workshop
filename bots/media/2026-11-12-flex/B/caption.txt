1. SUPPLE: Laozi wrote that we start life soft and end it stiff. Softness goes with life (retold).
2. STIFFEST: The stiffest men were linked with about 1.9 times the death risk of the most flexible men. A link, not proof.
3. ARTERIES: From age 40 up, a shorter reach went with stiffer arteries (the tubes that carry blood from your heart). A link only.
4. PRESSURE: After 8 weeks of stretching, the top blood pressure number went from 146 to 140, in one small trial.
5. HIP: Older people with repeated falls opened the hip less when walking. A link, not a cause.
6. AGE: Joseph Pilates believed your spine shows your real age.
7. INCH: A lovely Chinese saying, but no old source was found, and nobody measured that number.
8. STRETCHED: People who said they stretched were linked with about 10% lower odds of dying, other exercise counted.
9. BED: Women 40 to 61 stretched 10 minutes before bed for 3 weeks, and their menopause symptom score fell.
10. FIRST: An old yoga book puts asana first, for steadiness, health and light limbs.
