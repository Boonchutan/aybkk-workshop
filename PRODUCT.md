# Product

## Register

product

(Orientation pages override to brand per task: they are posters for a city workshop. Tools, journals, dashboards stay product.)

## Users

- China workshop students: on phones, usually inside WeChat's browser, on mainland networks. They read Chinese first, some English. They open the orientation once before a workshop and the practice journal often after it.
- Bangkok shala students: en/th, phones, drop-ins and regulars.
- Russia workshop students: ru pages, same phone-first pattern.
- Boonchu, Jamsai and staff: internal tools (dashboard, faces, calendar, send-links) on laptop and phone.

## Product Purpose

The student-facing infrastructure of the AYBKK workshop business: orientation pages that welcome and inform before each city workshop, a practice journal that keeps students connected to the practice (and to AYBKK) after it, plus internal tracking tools. It is part of the China High-Ticket funnel: the polish level must match a premium, personal teaching brand.

## Brand Personality

Hand-made, playful-disciplined, personal. The brand is Boonchu's own ink: he draws the tour tees, the mascot bird, the lettering. Student-facing words stay plain: facts, times, one honest line. Never salesy, never corporate.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards and wellness-startup pastels.
- AI-looking gradient-hero landing pages, crafted taglines.
- Corporate yoga-chain gloss (Pure, CorePower style).
- Anything that could not have come from a person with a marker.

## Design Principles

1. The theme is the shirt. Each city workshop's identity comes from its tour tee: one strong color, the rest constant. Bangkok is the home shirt (walnut wood).
2. Boonchu's hand, not a template. Real tee art, handwritten notes, stickers, hand-drawn frames. If an element could ship on any SaaS, redraw it.
3. Poster loud, notebook quiet. Orientation pages get drenched in the shirt color; journals and tools stay on quiet paper with the shirt color only in accents. Long Chinese reading always wins.
4. China-first delivery. Fonts from the Google China mirror with instant system fallbacks; nothing may block on a font or an external asset. Phone-first, 480px containers.
5. One school, many rooms. Four color roles (shirt, wall, chalk, ink) and shared components keep every city recognizably AYBKK.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Body text contrast at 4.5:1 or better on every theme; chalk-on-shirt checked per city; light shirts flip art to dark ink.
- prefers-reduced-motion disables draw-on and wiggle animations.
- zh / en / th / ru all first-class; language never depends on webfont downloads.
- Touch-first: hit areas 44px+, no hover-only affordances.
