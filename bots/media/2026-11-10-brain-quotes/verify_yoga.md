Result: 4 confirmed (2, 4, 5, 6), 4 corrected (1, 3, 7, 10), 2 rejected (8 Yoga Sutra 2.48 and 9 Shvetashvatara 2.13: words are real and exact, but they do not say the theme).
No line is in `used.txt`. Same authors are there with OTHER lines only. One near-repeat: the SECOND half of the Hatha Yoga Pradipika asana verse is used ("It should be practised for gaining steady posture, health and lightness of body"); candidate 5 is the first half of that same verse.
Every English wording was checked in a scan I opened myself, a different scan from the researcher's where one exists. 9 of 10 were also read as a whole page: 7 as a page image of the scan (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9) and 2 as a PDF page (7, 10). Sanskrit was checked in a second copy (GRETIL) for 5 of 6; Sushruta only on sa.wikisource plus two Sanskrit dictionaries that cite the verse.
