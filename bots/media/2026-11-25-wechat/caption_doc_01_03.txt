02_suptaurdhva_CN: Supta Urdhva Pada Vajrasana

POST CAPTION

(This asana is called Supta Urdhva Pada Vajrasana. It comes near the end of the second series (intermediate).)

躺下，同侧的手从背后绕过去，抓住半 Padmasana 的那只脚。
(Lie down. The hand on the same side reaches around behind your back and holds the foot that is in half Padmasana.)

另一只手抓住另一只脚，然后滚起来坐好，再扭转。
(The other hand takes hold of the other foot, then you roll up to sit, and twist.)

这里面是有技巧的。

这就是老师在垫子上会教你的那种细节。

03_galavasana_CN: Galavasana

POST CAPTION

这是 Galavasana，三序列（高级A）里的一个体式。
(This is Galavasana, an asana from the third series (Advanced A).)

一排同学从头倒立落下进入体式，停留，再回到头倒立。
(A row of students comes down from headstand into the asana, holds, then goes back up to headstand.)

老话说得好：四两拨千斤。
(As the old saying goes: four ounces can deflect a thousand pounds.)

小小的巧劲，胜过很大的力气。
(A little clever force beats a lot of strength.)

高难度体式也是这个道理。
(Hard asanas follow the same principle.)

靠的是你的关节和呼吸，不是蛮力。
(It relies on your joints and your breath, not brute force.)

这段是在我们广州工作坊的口令课上拍的。
(This was filmed in a led class at our Guangzhou workshop.)

口令课上，老师报数，所有人跟着同一个呼吸一起动。
(In a led class the teacher calls the count, and everyone moves together on the same breath.)

没有人一开始就练这个。
(Nobody practises this at the very start.)

从一序列开始，一天一天练上来。
(You start from the first series and build up, one day at a time.)
