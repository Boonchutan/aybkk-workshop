1. KICK: Attributed to Bruce Lee: fear the man who has practised one kick 10,000 times.
2. HEDGEHOG: Archilochus: the fox knows many things, but the hedgehog knows one big thing.
3. NOWHERE: Seneca: to be everywhere is to be nowhere.
4. CARVE: Xunzi: keep carving and never stop, and even metal and stone will give way.
5. HABIT: Will Durant, summing up Aristotle: excellence is not an act, but a habit.
6. STRENGTH: Emerson: concentration is the secret of strength.
7. BASKET: Mark Twain: put all your eggs in the one basket, and watch that basket.
8. NOW: Marcus Aurelius told himself to concentrate on what is in front of him.
9. 73: Hokusai felt he was only starting to understand at 73.
10. LONG: Patanjali, Yoga Sutra 1.14: practice holds firm when it's done for a long time, without a break, with devotion.
