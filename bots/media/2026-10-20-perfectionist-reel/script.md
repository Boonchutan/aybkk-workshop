# Reel: 3 things for a perfectionist (Boonchu's own script, Plato light edit, 20 Oct 2026)

Toolkit: Triple Hook + numbered list of 3 tips. Spoken lines in his English.

HOOK
1. If you practise yoga and you are a perfectionist... these 3 simple things will make your practice click.
2. Many people practise Ashtanga every day. And still the body feel not organized. The practice feel... unfinished.
3. But when you use these 3 things, you progress. And you understand your body much better.

PAYOFF
- One. Stay inside your mat. Use every inch of it, with a plan. Head, arms, feet... never go outside the mat. In every asana. From start to finish.
- Two. Your hands stay in the same place. Chaturanga, upward dog, downward dog. Same place.
- Three. Pause. At least 1 second, in every vinyasa movement.

CLOSE
Even after many years of practice, these simple things organize your practice a lot. Think of it like this... practise with precision.
