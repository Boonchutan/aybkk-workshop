Bar to Mysore Room

One night at a bar explains every marketing word. Here is each word, what it looks like at AYBKK, and how our own strategies fix it.

The Reel tells one night at a bar in steps: be seen, get a hello, keep the talk going, get a yes, and stay together. AYBKK walks the same steps on two paths. Path 1 is the Bangkok shala: a Reel or a Google search, our Instagram profile, a LINE chat, a first morning, a pass, then a renewal; Path 2 is In-Depth Mysore, the 180,000 baht program (we call it 180K): a Reel or the Monday letter (our free weekly letter), a DM (a private message), a one-page plan, an application with a practice video, a call, then payment.

Someone sees a Reel or our Google listing and then acts: opens the profile, comments 'aybkk' to get the link, or taps Book or LINE on a /start page (the first page people land on from a link). Low click-through means they liked what they saw and scrolled on.
