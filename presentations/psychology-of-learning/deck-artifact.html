Motor learning · attention & practice · 运动学习
  • External focus beats internal. Cue the effect, not the body part — "press the floor away," not "engage your quads." 把注意力放在动作的效果上，比放在肌肉上更有效。 Wulf, most-replicated
  • Short daily beats occasional long. Distributed practice > massed. The daily Mysore format already fits this. 每天短时间练习，胜过偶尔的长时间。
  • Blocked for novices, varied for experts. Random practice feels worse today but sticks better later — but high variation overwhelms beginners. 新手先重复，老手再变化。 contextual interference
Feedback · the guidance hypothesis · 反馈
  • Fade your corrections as skill grows. Constant feedback creates dependence; less feedback = worse today, better learning. Don't correct every student on every pose every day. 随着进步，减少纠正，让学生自己感受体式。
  • Keep feedback on the task, not the person. ~1/3 of feedback interventions make performance worse — usually when aimed at the self. "The hand goes here," not "you always get this wrong." 反馈针对动作，不针对人。 Kluger & DeNisi, 607 effects
Self-efficacy · Bandura's four sources · 自我效能
Confidence is built, in order of power:
  • 1 · Mastery — actually succeeding. By far the strongest. Scale postures so students genuinely achieve them. 自信最强的来源是真实的成功。
  • 2 · Vicarious — seeing relatable others succeed (not just advanced demos)
  • 3 · Verbal — credible encouragement (helps, can't replace success)
  • 4 · Emotional — teach students to read shaking & fatigue as normal, not failure
Autonomy & expectancies · OPTIMAL theory · 自主与预期
  • Set them up to expect success. Honest positive framing improves performance and learning — not just kindness. 让学生相信"能做到"，表现和学习都更好。
  • Give small choices within a frame. Which variation, how many breaths, when to ask. Self-controlled practice learns better. Natural in Mysore. 给学生一定的掌控，学得更好。
  • Cue externally. Together these three stack — and they're free. Wulf & Lewthwaite, 2016
Motivation & dropout · 坚持与流失
  • The first 3 months are the danger zone. ~63% of new members quit before month 3; attendance studies show ~50%. Treat the first 90 days as a retention emergency. 前三个月最容易放弃。头90天重点建立关系、成功感、习惯。 Sperandei 2016
  • Habit takes ~66 days, not 21 — and missing a day is fine. Set this expectation to prevent early discouragement. 养成习惯平均约66天，漏掉一天没关系。 Lally 2010
  • Use if-then plans + a simple practice log. "After I wake, I roll out my mat." Tie practice to a fixed cue, time, place. 用"如果…就…"计划，并记录练习。
The teacher's blind spots · 老师的盲点
  • You underestimate beginner difficulty by ~2×. Expertise makes predictions worse. Don't trust "this is easy" — watch beginners and ask them directly. 老师会严重低估新手的难度（约两倍）。别相信直觉，多观察多问。 Hinds 1999; expert blind spot
  • The expertise-reversal effect. Heavy step-by-step guidance that helps a novice holds back an experienced student — the extra detail is now load. Fade it. 对新手有用的详细指导，对老手反而有害。
  • Novices: full demos, few cues. Working memory is limited — break it into small steps, don't pile on cues. cognitive load
Talking to prospects · don't sell · 与潜在学员
  • Motivational interviewing beats persuasion. Ask open questions, listen, reflect their own reasons back, ask permission before advising. Let them talk themselves toward the decision. 用"动机式访谈"：提问、倾听、反映他们自己的理由，而不是推销。 Rubak 2005, 72 trials
  • Pressure backfires — reactance. Threaten someone's freedom to choose and they push back. "It's completely your decision" — and mean it. 越推越抗拒。保护对方的选择自由。
  • The relationship predicts the outcome. The alliance is a primary driver, not secondary to technique. Flückiger 2018, 295 studies
Chinese learners · specifically · 中国学生
  • Repetition is a route to understanding, not shallow. These students link memorizing and understanding — disciplined repetition of the sequence fits both Ashtanga and their tradition. 重复练习序列，对他们是通向理解的路径。
  • Protect face (面子). Correct privately & gently. Don't expose incompetence in front of peers; normalize difficulty for the whole group. The one-on-one Mysore format suits this. 面子很重要。尽量私下纠正，不要当众让学生难堪。
  • Autonomy = volition, not independence. A respected teacher guiding choices enhances motivation here. Emphasize competence — real success — heavily. "自主"指学生认同练习，而非脱离老师。可信任的老师引导选择，同样激发动力。
Drop these · popular but unsupported · 该丢掉的迷思
Learning styles (visual/auditory)→no credible evidence
Growth mindset transforms results→real but tiny (~1% of variance)
21 days to a habit→median ~66 days (18–254)
10,000 hours makes an expert→Ericsson rejected it; practice ≈ 18% in sport
Left-brain / right-brain learners→no evidence (1,011 brain scans)
More feedback is always better→>1/3 of feedback makes it worse
学习风格、强版成长型思维、21天习惯、一万小时定律、左右脑分型——都缺乏科学依据。
