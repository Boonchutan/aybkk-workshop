# Google review QR card (6 Nov 2026, Neo)

The QR opens https://aybkk.net/review, our own short link that redirects to Google's
"write a review" page for the AYBKK place. If the Google place ever changes, fix the
redirect in server.js; the printed cards keep working.

- aybkk-review-card-A6.pdf / .png: desk card, 105 x 148 mm, print at 100% (no "fit to page")
- aybkk-review-phone-1080x1920.png: for the door tablet or a screen
- aybkk-review-qr-only.png: the code alone, for other designs
- make.js: rebuilds all three (fonts from public/fonts on main: longcang, patrickhand, worksans, plexthai-th)

Google rules (carnegie.md 15 Oct): no reward of any kind, ask EVERY student (not only happy ones),
never ask for a star number. The card follows them. Thai lines need Jamsai's check before printing.
In LINE, send the link (aybkk.net/review), not the picture: nobody can scan their own screen.
