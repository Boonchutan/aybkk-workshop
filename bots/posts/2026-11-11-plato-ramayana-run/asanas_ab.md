# Advanced A and Advanced B, exactly as printed in Boonchu's PDF

Read on 11 Nov 2026 from the owner's own file. This replaces the Advanced C and D search (asanas_cd.md): the PDF has no C or D.

## Source

- "Ashtanga Yoga Asana Practise Sheets, Part 2: Intermediate, Advanced A, Advanced B Series", Philippa Asher, www.ashtangaphilippa.com, "Copyright (c) Philippa Asher 2016".
- File: pdf/asana-sheets-part-2.pdf, 10 pages, every page is one picture (150 dpi), no text layer.
- Printed page numbers (top left): cover (no number, it is p.11), p.12 Notes, p.13 and p.14 Intermediate, **p.15 "First Half of Advanced A Series"**, **p.16 "Second Half of Advanced A Series"**, **p.17 "First Half of Advanced B Series"**, **p.18 "Second Half of Advanced B Series"**, p.19 Pranayama, p.20 Meditation.
- How it was read: the four page pictures, then every label strip cut out and enlarged (out/crops_ab/). Every name below is copied letter by letter from the enlarged label.
- Word meanings: Monier-Williams Sanskrit dictionary (Cologne online), marked MW. Files in out/dl_mw_ab/ and out/dl_mw/.
- Ramayana ties: Valmiki Ramayana, valmikiramayan.net (kanda and sarga numbers), files in out/txt_bala, txt_ayodhya, txt_aranya, txt_ab. Only ties I saw in the text are marked CHECKED.

## What the sheet prints around the asanas

- Header on p.15, p.16, p.18: "Dristi nose, unless otherwise stated / Asanas held for 5 breaths / Preparations & exits are transitional". Header on p.17 (with its typing slips): "Dristi nose, unless otherwsie stated / Aanas held for 5 breaths".
- p.15: "* = arm balance preparation & exit" (the star sits beside a headstand on the hands, and under each arm balance that uses it).
- Key (p.12): (S) = Samasthitih, RLS = repeat LEFT side, (F) = Full Vinyasa, (H) = Half Vinyasa, (C) = Chakrasana, (HS) = Half Vinyasa from Samasthitih, (CS) = Chakrasana to Samasthitih, (HC) = Half Vinyasa from Chakorasana.
- p.12 Notes: "Most of the Advanced Series arm balances, use "Mukta Hasta Sirsasna A" as a preparation and exit, with a half vinyasa between postures." And: a few asanas "towards the end of Advanced A, do not take a Vinyasa Sequence between sides or postures eg: from "Digasana", to "Trivikramasana", to "Natarajasana"."

Columns: name as printed (with any bracket note printed under it) | dristi printed | code printed beside the photo (my match of code to photo is by position) | meaning | shape in the photo | named after + Ramayana tie.
