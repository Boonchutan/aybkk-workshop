## DO NOT SAY / DO NOT DO
1. No actor, director, producer or studio name. No "starring".
2. No review: not "good", "bad", "beautiful", "you must see it", "I liked it". Do not say he has seen it unless he tells us he has.
3. Never "like in the movie" or "the movie shows". The film differs from Valmiki in places (fact 11) and we have not seen it. Our stories come from Valmiki with kanda and sarga. Say "the old book says".
4. No picture, clip, trailer, poster, music or cinema recording from the film. There is a court order (C7). No film hashtag that looks like an official tie-in, no "official", no "collab".
5. Never the slogan "Jai Shri Ram" and never the film's taglines. Never call the Ramayana "history", "true history" or "only a myth". Say "old story" or "old book".
6. Nothing about politics, parties, temples, tax-free, protests, religious groups, who is offended, the Ravana argument or Sita fighting. Do not answer comments on these.
7. No budget, box office, record, "most expensive" or running-time number.
8. Do not say it is in cinemas in Russia or in China. For RU and ZH captions keep "in Bangkok". Hong Kong only from 12 Nov.
9. No date for the next film (2027 or 2028, sources differ). No "trilogy". No spoilers of the film's ending or the after-credits scene.
10. From the brief, still in force: Indian names only, never "King Rama", no Thai kings, no Ramakien scenes, no joke about Rama, Sita, Lakshmana, Hanuman or any sage.
11. Old articles give 6 Nov or 8 Nov. If a date is ever needed: 5 Nov 2026. Better to say no date at all.
