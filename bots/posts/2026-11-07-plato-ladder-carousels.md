# The Ladder of Life: 3 carousels (7 Nov 2026)

Cover + 9 steps each. Big word = a feeling. Every fact and quote checked with a source.

## Set A · The Ladder of Words (9 cultures)

**Cover · READY**  
Nobody skips a step of life, not even the healthiest. You only choose how READY you arrive.  
*9 steps · swipe*

**1 · WONDER**  
Life starts in WONDER, before you can do a single thing. Plato, the Greek thinker, wrote: "philosophy begins in wonder." Philosophy means the love of wisdom.  
*Plato · Theaetetus · tr. Benjamin Jowett*

**2 · JOY**  
Children learn to move through JOY, not rules. Thais say: "ไม้อ่อนดัดง่าย ไม้แก่ดัดยาก" (young wood is easy to bend, old wood is hard to bend). Old wood still bends, just slower.  
*Thai proverb (สำนวน) · Thai Wiktionary · our translation*

**3 · PROUD**  
Real strength makes you PROUD because you built it yourself, one small day at a time. A Swahili proverb: "Haba na haba hujaza kibaba" (little by little fills up the measure).  
*Swahili proverb, East Africa · Swahili Proverbs, University of Illinois*

**4 · FRAGILE**  
Nobody is too strong to feel FRAGILE. Sickness visits every life. Susan Sontag wrote: "Everyone who is born holds dual citizenship," one with the healthy, one with the sick.  
*Susan Sontag · Illness as Metaphor, 1978*

**5 · HOPE**  
Healing has its own pace, but HOPE grows each time you get back up. The Japanese say: "七転び八起き" (fall down seven times, get up eight). Your mat waits for you.  
*Japanese proverb · Digital Daijisen (Shogakukan) · our translation*

**6 · CURIOUS**  
A CURIOUS mind is strength that does not need muscles. The Persian poet Ferdowsi told readers to "roam earth, converse with every one." Keep asking.  
*Ferdowsi · Shahnameh, c. 1010 · tr. A. G. and E. Warner, 1905*

**7 · HUMBLE**  
Wise people stay HUMBLE: the more they know, the more they listen. A Korean proverb: "벼는 익을수록 고개를 숙인다" (the riper the rice plant, the more it bends its head).  
*Korean proverb (속담) · tr. Wiktionary*

**8 · GRATEFUL**  
Later in life, you feel GRATEFUL you kept moving. Russians say: "Береги платье снову, а здоровье смолоду" (care for clothes while new, health while young). You will never be younger than today.  
*Russian proverb · V. I. Dal, Proverbs of the Russian People, 1862 · our translation*

**9 · PEACE**  
Every body slows, and a long life can still end in PEACE, like a leaf going home. A Chinese saying: "落叶归根" (fallen leaves will return to the roots). Thank your body.  
*Chinese idiom · originally 葉落歸根, Jingde Record of the Lamp, c. 1004 · tr. Wiktionary*

**Caption**

Nobody skips a step of life, not even the healthiest.

A moving life does not let you skip one. It lets you meet each one stronger and calmer.

Which step are you on right now?

## Set B · One Life: B.K.S. Iyengar (1918 to 2014)

**Cover · HOPE**  
A healthy life does not skip the hard steps. HOPE is climbing each one stronger.  
*One life, 1918 to 2014 · 10 steps · swipe*

**1 · FRAGILE**  
The man who helped take yoga to the world began life FRAGILE. B.K.S. Iyengar was born sick in 1918. As a boy he had malaria every year, plus typhoid and TB.  
*B.K.S. Iyengar to Harsh Desai · The Tribune, 2005 (retold) · AP, 2014*

**2 · DOUBT**  
Even his own teacher looked at him with DOUBT. He started yoga at 15, and his body was very stiff. He kept going anyway, practising for hours.  
*B.K.S. Iyengar to Harsh Desai · The Tribune, 2005 · Open magazine, 2014 · AP, 2014 (retold)*

**3 · BRAVE**  
The stiff boy grew strong, then BRAVE. Two years later he "could do a lot of backbends." At 18, when no other student would go to teach in Pune, he went.  
*B.K.S. Iyengar to Harsh Desai · The Tribune, 2005 · AP, 2014*

**4 · HURT**  
HURT comes even to the strongest body. At 60, his scooter hit a rock. "My spine was twisted." He was told he would never practise again.  
*B.K.S. Iyengar to Harsh Desai · The Tribune, 2005 · Open magazine, 2014*

**5 · PATIENT**  
Healing was PATIENT work, even for a master. He said he recovered "only very slowly." He had to learn yoga all over again, like a beginner.  
*B.K.S. Iyengar to Harsh Desai · The Tribune, 2005 · Open magazine, 2014*

**6 · CURIOUS**  
A CURIOUS mind kept him learning into his 80s. He taught himself much of his yoga by watching how the body works. At 86 he published another book, Light on Life.  
*B.K.S. Iyengar to Harsh Desai · The Tribune, 2005 · Open magazine, 2014 (retold)*

**7 · CALM**  
The wise old teacher was CALM about slow progress. He did not trust quick yoga on TV. One student, he said, took 11 years just to know the back of her knees.  
*Madhavankutty Pillai · Open magazine, 2014 (retold)*

**8 · STUBBORN**  
Staying strong late in life takes a STUBBORN habit. In his 70s he was too weak to climb stairs. Near 90, he still did three hours of asanas a day.  
*Madhavankutty Pillai · Open magazine, 2014 (retold)*

**9 · PEACE**  
The last step asks for PEACE, not strength. Even a strong body grows weak at the end. He practised until two months before he passed away, aged 95.  
*Associated Press · obituary, Aug 2014 (retold)*

**Caption**

Born sick. Hurt at 60. Too weak for stairs in his 70s. Still practising at 95.

Nobody skips a step of life, not even B.K.S. Iyengar.

At every step, he went back to his practice.

Which step are you on right now?

## Set C · Your Ladder (your voice, to the reader)

**Cover · CALM**  
Nobody skips a step of life, not even the healthy. What you choose is how strong and CALM you meet each one.  
*AYBKK · THE LADDER · 9 steps · swipe*

**1 · WONDER**  
WONDER was the first thing you were good at. As a baby you found your own feet and stared. In your practice, you can look at your body like that again.  
*AYBKK · THE LADDER · 1/9*

**2 · JOY**  
As a kid, you learned to move with JOY, one fall at a time. Nobody rushed your first steps. In Mysore class, the teacher gives the next asana when you are ready.  
*AYBKK · THE LADDER · 2/9*

**3 · PROUD**  
Strength you build slowly makes you PROUD in a quiet way. You cannot buy it. Same mat, same time, until one day you can do the asana you could not.  
*AYBKK · THE LADDER · 3/9*

**4 · FEAR**  
Even healthy people get sick, and FEAR comes with it. Practice does not stop sickness. But you have your breath to come back to, and a teacher who notices your empty mat.  
*AYBKK · THE LADDER · 4/9*

**5 · HOPE**  
Coming back starts with HOPE, not strength. You return slower, maybe weaker. But your mat and your practice time have not moved, so the way back is already there.  
*AYBKK · THE LADDER · 5/9*

**6 · CURIOUS**  
The strongest mind is not the smartest one, but the CURIOUS one. You learn your whole sequence by heart. Then a new asana comes, and you are a beginner again.  
*AYBKK · THE LADDER · 6/9*

**7 · HUMBLE**  
Years on the mat make you HUMBLE, and that is where wisdom starts. Some days the asana comes, some days it does not. You stop fighting the day you have.  
*AYBKK · THE LADDER · 7/9*

**8 · FREE**  
Later in life, strength means feeling FREE. Getting up from the floor on your own. Carrying your own bag. Keep more of it the way you built it: a little, every day.  
*AYBKK · THE LADDER · 8/9*

**9 · PEACE**  
When the body grows weak, the heart can still be at PEACE, grateful for the climb. Every practice ends the same way: lie still, breathe, let go.  
*AYBKK · THE LADDER · 9/9*

**Caption**

Nobody skips a step of life. Not even the healthy.

A daily practice does not stop age or sickness. It changes how you meet them: a little stronger, a little calmer.

Which step are you on now? Tell me the number.

#aybkk #ashtanga #ashtangabangkok #boonchutanti #mysore
