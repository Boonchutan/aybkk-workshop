# Hidden Stories of Ashtanga, Ep. 2 (Vritra part 2), v2

Toolkit pick: Triple Hook + /story, run as fail, hero, payoff.
Why: the whiplash is already inside the myth: he wanted a son to kill Indra, he got a son for Indra to kill. /story keeps one mistake: stress in the wrong place, in the chant and on the mat.

On screen from frame 1 (sound off): "One wrong sound made this demon."

## HOOK (Triple Hook)
This is how the demon who stole the rain was born.
His father made one small mistake.
Most of us make it on the mat.
He wanted a son to kill Indra.
He got a son for Indra to kill.

## CONTEXT (one line)
Two days ago, Indra, the god of thunder, killed this demon with his thunderbolt.

## FAIL (the myth, bedtime voice)
Tvashtr was the craftsman of the gods.
His son had three heads.
He was the gods' own priest.
But his mother's family were demons.
In public, he gave the offerings to the gods.
In secret, he gave some to the demons too.
Indra saw the danger.
His own priest was making his enemies strong.
So Indra cut off all three heads.
Tvashtr lost his son.
He lit a fire and chanted for a new one.
A son who would kill Indra.
The word was "Indrashatru."
Stress the end: "the killer of Indra."
Stress the start: "the one Indra kills."
Tvashtr stressed the start.
Vritra rose from the fire.
Huge, angry, born to destroy Indra.
And born to die by Indra's hand.

On screen: "indra-SHATRU = killer of Indra", then "INDRA-shatru = the one Indra kills".

## HERO (turn to the adults)
Tvashtr put the stress in the wrong place.
We do it too.
In Ashtanga, Indra's thunderbolt comes back.
Supta Vajrasana, the sleeping thunderbolt.
Legs in lotus.
Reach behind your back and hold your toes.
Drop back until the top of your head touches the floor.
Now come up.
If you crunch your abs, and pull up with your neck,
your hands let go.
And it fails.
Put the stress in your chest.
Hold your bandhas, and breathe.
You come up in one piece.
Then it makes you strong.

On screen (small): "Intermediate Series. Learn it with your teacher."

## PAYOFF (closes the hook)
Same asana. Where you put the stress decides the ending.
Tvashtr got one chance.
You get a new one every morning.

## Notes
- Length: about 80 to 90 seconds. For a true 60 s, cut the three-heads part to one line and save it for its own episode.
- Cut to save time (put back if you want): "One drank soma, one drank wine, one ate food." / "The three heads turned into three birds." / "His ending was inside his own name."
- Why Indra acted: Taittiriya Samhita 2.5.1 (Keith): Indra was afraid Vishvarupa was "diverting the sovereignty" from him. Bhagavata Purana 6.9.4: afraid of defeat by the demons. Script says "Indra saw the danger" (paraphrase).
- Accent: end accent (tatpurusha) = killer of Indra; start accent (bahuvrihi) = whose killer is Indra. Macdonell, Vedic Grammar; SB 1.6.3.10; Paniniya Shiksha 52.
- Subtitles: Chinese 把重音放错了地方 (the chant) / 把力用错了地方 (the asana). Thai ลงน้ำหนัก works for both.
