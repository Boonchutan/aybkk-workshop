The Five Kleshas

The Roots of Suffering 五种烦恼 · 苦的根源
"Heyaṃ duḥkham anāgatam" — future suffering can be avoided.
尚未到来的苦，可以避免。 — Yoga Sutra II.16
The Root · 根源

Pain in practice is not just physical —
the mind fuels it.

练习中的痛，不只是身体的——是心在助燃。
Knee pain 膝痛→ frustration→ tight breath→ more pain
Avidyā is the field. The other four are its crop. 无明是土壤，其余四种，是长在其上的作物。 (YS 2.4)
Avidyāअविद्या无明
Ignorance · the root 无知 · 根
Mistaking the impermanent for the permanent — the not-Self for the Self. (YS 2.5)
The tiger cub raised among goats — bleating, grazing, afraid — until it sees its reflection, and roars.一只在羊群中长大的虎，直到看见水中的倒影，才想起自己是谁。
A whole life lived weak — not from any real limit, but from a false name.
In practice: "I can't do this posture = I am not enough." 「我做不到 = 我不够好」
Svādhyāya — self-study · presence · viveka 自我研习 · 临在 · 明辨
Asmitāअस्मिता我执
Egoism · I-am-ness 自我
Confusing the Seer with the instrument of seeing — the manufactured "I." (YS 2.6)
Rāvaṇa — scholar, devotee, king — was so proud he never named a human as a threat in his boon. A "mere human," Rāma, ended him.罗波那博学而虔诚，却因傲慢，求庇佑时不屑将「人」列为威胁；终为凡人罗摩所灭。
It was not his power, but his ego, that authored his ruin.
In practice: "I know, I know. I am strong. I am flexible. I am not flexible." 「我懂。我很强。我很柔软。我不够柔软。」
Humility in repetition · gratitude in surrender 重复中谦卑 · 臣服中感恩
Rāgaराग贪
Attachment · craving 执着
Clinging to pleasure — organizing life around repeating it. (YS 2.7)
King Yayāti took his son's youth and indulged a thousand years — and found desire is never quenched by enjoyment; it flares, like fire fed with ghee.亚雅提王借来儿子的青春，纵欲千年，终于明白：欲望不因享受而止，反如浇了酥油的火，越烧越旺。
A thousand extra years of pleasure left him emptier than before.
In practice: "I want that depth, that old jump, yesterday's practice." 「我想找回那个深度、那一跳、昨天的练习。」
Remember impermanence · enjoy what comes, let go when it goes 忆无常 · 来则享，去则放
Dveṣaद्वेष嗔
Aversion · dislike 厌恶
Recoiling from pain — the mirror of craving. (YS 2.8)
Śiśupāla nursed a lifelong hatred of Krishna. At the great sacrifice he hurled his hundredth insult — and the Sudarśana-chakra took his head.童护王一生仇恨黑天；大典之上，他骂出第一百句，善见神轮当即取其首级。
His hatred drove him into the very ruin he wished on another. It eats the hater.
In practice: avoiding backbends, twists, early mornings — laziness and excuses. 逃避后弯、扭转、清晨——懒惰与借口。
Steady breath through challenge — Dveṣa becomes the doorway 于挑战中稳住呼吸 · 嗔成门户
Abhiniveśāअभिनिवेश怖畏
Clinging to life · fear of death 贪生
The will-to-live and dread of ending — rooted even in the wise. (YS 2.9)
Sent to Death, the boy Nachiketā refused every bribe — long life, gold, dancing girls — and kept only his question. He won the Self that death cannot touch.少年纳奇凯塔被送到死神门前，拒绝一切诱惑，只守着他的问题，终得那死亡无法触及的「真我」。
Aesop's dog lunges at its reflection for a second bone — and drops the real one. The grip causes the loss it fears.
In practice: panic in deep asana, breath-holds, transitions. 深度体式、屏息、过渡中的惊慌。
You are not the body · follow the task at hand · one step at a time 你非此身 · 专注眼前 · 一次一步
A caution for the advanced · 给资深练习者
A dormant seed is not a dead seed. 潜伏的种子，不是死去的种子。

Dormant

潜伏

a seed only — until conditions water it

Attenuated

减弱

thinned by practice, but still there

Interrupted

间断

overridden, flickering on and off

Active

活跃

running the show at full strength

"The kleshas are not enemies. They are messengers." 烦恼不是敌人，而是信使。
Kriyā yoga — tapas · svādhyāya · īśvara-praṇidhāna
Pratipakṣa bhāvana — cultivate the opposite
Viveka — discernment
To Close · 收束
Not the wall in your practice —
the doorway,
carved with your own name.
不是练习中的墙——
是门，门上刻着你自己的名字。
