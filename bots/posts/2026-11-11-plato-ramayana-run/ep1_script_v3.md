The Teacher Who Went Silent
You practice today and nothing happened... yet.
In the old Ramayana, two boys guard their teacher's fire ceremony. Priests chant mantra around the fire. Six nights, no sleep. The teacher keeps a vow of silence.
Five days, nothing comes. On day six the monsters come down like storm clouds. One arrow sends one far into the sea. Rama stopped the other monsters too.
The five quiet days were the practice.
