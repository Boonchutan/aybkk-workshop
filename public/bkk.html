Your teacher

Boonchu Tanti
Authorized Level 2 by R. Sharath Jois

Boonchu is a former mechanical engineer. He found Ashtanga yoga in 2008, while living in the US.

He has studied with Sharath Jois in Mysore since 2008. He hosted R. Sharath Jois in Bangkok in 2018 and 2024.

In 2013 he opened AYBKK in his hometown, Bangkok. He also created The Yoga Comics, a Facebook page about the eight limbs of yoga. AYBKK has been the home shala of Bangkok’s Ashtanga community since 2013. Several of Bangkok’s Ashtanga teachers practised and assisted here before opening their own shalas.

His teaching looks at how each body works, and at the student's own commitment. Every body is different, and so is every practice.

What is Mysore style?

  • It is self-practice. You move through the Ashtanga sequence at your own pace, with your own breath.
  • The teacher walks the room and gives each student individual adjustments.
  • Beginners are welcome. We teach you from zero.
  • This is the traditional way Ashtanga is taught, as in Mysore, India.

Your first class

  • New to Mysore style? Start with a monthly package, so the teacher can guide you step by step. Drop-in is for visitors who already know the sequence.
  • Plan to practise at least 4 days a week, and book your classes in advance.
  • Bring your own mat and a towel, and wear clothes you can move in. Come early, so you have time to settle in and talk to the teacher.
  • No phones, no photos and no talking in the shala during practice.
  • After practice, clean your mat and the floor around it with the spray we provide.
  • The shala is closed on full moon and new moon days.
