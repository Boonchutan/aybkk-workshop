# See you tomorrow: messages for quiet students

**Status:** English APPROVED by Boonchu (14 Nov 2026, "OK, use the defaults for quiet students"). Thai: draft until Jamsai reads it. Staff copy and send by hand on LINE. Nothing is sent by the system.

**Decisions (Boonchu, 14 Nov 2026, all defaults):**
- First note: M, by hand from the shala LINE (Jamsai when M is off), at most one per student in 30 days. Second note: Boonchu in his own name (Jamsai in her name in a week he is away). No third note, ever.
- Day counts: membership longer than 3 months 7 and 14; 3 months or less 5 and 10; class pack one note at 14; first pass never used: day 3 (membership), day 7 (pack). From 21 quiet days with no note yet: Boonchu only.
- First note opens with "See you tomorrow?".
- The end date stays on the staff screen, never in a note.
- Rezerv packages stay off the list until the switch day.
- "Sick or hurt" is saved as a category only, no details, never in LINE or a sheet.
- The Today box and the 05:00 brief section ship OFF. Switch on after Jamsai reads the Thai and the Rezerv switch day; the brief section only after 2 weeks of the Today box with no wrong flag.

**All Thai: needs Jamsai's read.** The Thai judge wrote the rewrites as a reader and said no Thai speaker on the team has read them. Points for her are at the end.

**Placeholders:** `{name}` = first name only (Thai uses คุณ{name}; staff may change it to พี่ or น้อง). `{ka}` = ครับ or ค่ะ of the person who SENDS. It only follows a statement, never a question or นะ. English has no `{ka}`.

**Five checks before any send**
1. Open the student. Is the last visit right? If a booking has no check-in, ask the teacher first.
2. Look at LINE and the inbox. If they are waiting on a reply from us, answer that and send no note.
3. Did they already tell us (leave, sick, travel)? Tap "We know why" and send nothing.
4. Is the pass still live, and is tomorrow an open day? (The draft changes by itself when tomorrow is closed.)
5. Can you keep the promise in the draft (book the class, tell the teacher)? If not, do not send it.

One note per step. No second note when there is no reply.

- **EN:** {name}, see you tomorrow? We have not seen you at the shala for a few days, so we are saying hello. Come at the time that suits you.
- **TH (needs Jamsai's read):** สวัสดี{ka} คุณ{name} ไม่ได้เจอกันที่ชาลาหลายวันแล้ว เลยทักมาหา{ka} ถ้าพรุ่งนี้สะดวก แล้วเจอกันที่ชาลา{ka}

**When tomorrow is a moon day or closed day** (key `firstClosed`)
- **EN:** Hi {name}, AYBKK here. We have not seen you at the shala for a few days, so we are saying hello. Come at the time that suits you.
- **TH (needs Jamsai's read):** สวัสดี{ka} คุณ{name} ไม่ได้เจอกันที่ชาลาหลายวันแล้ว เลยทักมาหา{ka} มาฝึกตามเวลาที่สะดวกได้เลย แล้วเจอกันที่ชาลา{ka}

## 2. Teacher's note. Still quiet at 14 days (10 on a pass of 3 months or less)
- **Who sends:** Boonchu, in his own name, after he has seen the name. `{ka}` is ครับ. In a week he is away, Jamsai sends it in her own name and changes the first line. This is the last note.
- **Key:** `teacher`
- **EN:** {name}, Boonchu here. I have not seen you in the room for a while. No need to explain. How are you these days? The shala is here when you are ready.
- **TH (needs Jamsai's read):** คุณ{name} ครูบุญชูเอง{ka} ไม่ได้เจอกันในห้องฝึกมาสักพักแล้ว เลยทักมาหา{ka} ช่วงนี้เป็นยังไงบ้าง ถ้ามีอะไรติดขัด บอกครูได้เลย พร้อมเมื่อไหร่ก็มาฝึกได้ แล้วเจอกัน{ka}

## 3. First pass, never came (day 3 on a membership, day 7 on a class pack)
- **Who sends:** M or Jamsai. Only for a student who has really never practised with us. A known student with a new pass gets message 1.
- **Key:** `fresh`
- **EN:** Hi {name}, welcome to AYBKK. Your pass is open. Shall I book your first morning? Tell me the day and I will tell the teacher you are coming. If it is your first time, we teach from the beginning.
- **TH (needs Jamsai's read):** สวัสดี{ka} คุณ{name} ยินดีต้อนรับ{ka} แพ็กเกจของคุณ{name}เริ่มใช้ได้แล้ว จองเช้าแรกให้เลยดีไหม บอกวันที่สะดวกมาได้เลย เดี๋ยวจองคลาสให้ แล้วจะบอกครูไว้ให้ด้วย{ka} ถ้าเป็นครั้งแรก เราสอนตั้งแต่เริ่มต้น{ka}

## 4. Quiet, and the pass ends within 14 days (used in place of message 1)
- **Who sends:** M or Jamsai (Boonchu for a long-pass holder he knows well). No renewal talk. The end date stays on the staff screen; give it plainly if they ask.
- **Key:** `ending`
- **EN:** {name}, see you tomorrow? Your pass is still open. Come at the time that suits you and start from where you are.
- **TH (needs Jamsai's read):** สวัสดี{ka} คุณ{name} แพ็กเกจของคุณ{name}ยังใช้ได้อยู่ มาฝึกตามเวลาที่สะดวกได้เลย{ka} ค่อย ๆ เริ่มใหม่ได้ ถ้าพรุ่งนี้สะดวก แล้วเจอกันที่ชาลา{ka}

**When tomorrow is closed** (key `endingClosed`)
- **EN:** Hi {name}. Your pass is still open. Come at the time that suits you and start from where you are.
- **TH (needs Jamsai's read):** สวัสดี{ka} คุณ{name} แพ็กเกจของคุณ{name}ยังใช้ได้อยู่ มาฝึกตามเวลาที่สะดวกได้เลย{ka} ค่อย ๆ เริ่มใหม่ได้ แล้วเจอกันที่ชาลา{ka}

A member who came a little and is not quiet gets no message. Staff see the tag "ends soon, came little" and the teacher says a word in the room.

## 5. When they answer (one reply, same day, in normal reply hours; then quiet)

### 5a. Work or travel (key `replyBusy`)
- **Who sends:** whoever got the reply.
- **EN:** Thank you for telling me, {name}. That sounds like a busy time. There is nothing to catch up on here. Come at the time that suits you. If you tell me the day, the teacher will know you are coming.
- **TH (needs Jamsai's read):** ขอบคุณที่เล่าให้ฟัง{ka} คุณ{name} ช่วงนี้คงยุ่งมากเลย กลับมาเมื่อไหร่ก็ฝึกต่อได้เลย มาฝึกตามเวลาที่สะดวก{ka} จะมาวันไหนบอกไว้ก่อนได้ จะบอกครูไว้ให้{ka}
- Long trip: ask M about the freeze rule first. Do not promise it in the message.

### 5b. Sick or hurt (key `replyHealth`)
- **Who sends:** whoever got the reply. Tell Boonchu the same day.
- **EN:** Thank you for telling me, {name}. I hope you feel better soon. Take the time you need. When you come back, tell the teacher how you are before you start. We will go at your pace.
- **TH (needs Jamsai's read):** ขอบคุณที่บอก{ka} คุณ{name} ขอให้หายไว ๆ {ka} พร้อมเมื่อไหร่ค่อยกลับมาได้เลย ก่อนเริ่มฝึกเล่าให้ครูฟังสักนิดว่าเป็นยังไง แล้วเราค่อย ๆ ไปด้วยกัน{ka}
- A wish, never a promise. No advice about the body. No further message until they write.

### 5c. "I lost the habit" (key `replyHabit`)
- **Who sends:** whoever got the reply.
- **EN:** Thank you for saying it, {name}. There is nothing to make up for and nothing to explain. A habit starts again with one morning. Which morning suits you? Tell me and the teacher will know you are coming.
- **TH (needs Jamsai's read):** ขอบคุณที่บอกตรง ๆ {ka} คุณ{name} ไม่เป็นไรเลย เริ่มใหม่ได้เสมอ มาสักเช้าเดียวก็เริ่มได้แล้ว{ka} เช้าวันไหนสะดวก บอกไว้ได้เลย จะบอกครูไว้ให้{ka}
- When they name a day: book it, tell the teacher, then "See you tomorrow."

### 5d. Money (key `replyMoney`)
- **Who sends:** whoever got the reply. Anything about a price or a next pass goes to Boonchu. Use only while the pass is live.
- **EN:** Thank you for telling me, {name}. I understand, and you do not need to say more. Your pass is still open, so come at the time that suits you. We will be glad to see you at the shala.
- **TH (needs Jamsai's read):** ขอบคุณที่ไว้ใจเล่าให้ฟัง{ka} คุณ{name} เข้าใจเลย{ka} แพ็กเกจของคุณ{name}ยังใช้ได้อยู่ มาฝึกตามเวลาที่สะดวกได้เลย แล้วเจอกันที่ชาลา{ka}

## The 3 questions
Ask ONE, only after they have written back. Then be quiet. Never ask "why". Never a second question the same day. Yes, no and "not now" are all good answers.

1. **EN:** How have your mornings been lately?
   **TH (needs Jamsai's read):** ขอถามหน่อย{ka} ช่วงนี้ตอนเช้าเป็นยังไงบ้าง
   Finds what changed in their life (work hours, sleep, family).
2. **EN:** Does the practice time still fit your day?
   **TH (needs Jamsai's read):** เวลาฝึกตอนนี้ยังสะดวกอยู่ไหม ถ้าไม่สะดวก บอกได้เลย{ka}
   A yes or no question. Finds the easiest problem to fix.
3. **EN:** Is there anything at the shala or in the teaching you would like us to change?
   **TH (needs Jamsai's read):** มีอะไรที่ชาลาหรือในการสอนที่อยากให้เราปรับไหม บอกตรง ๆ ได้เลย{ka}
   Finds the reason students rarely say. Thank them, do not defend, and send the answer to Boonchu word for word.

## Never say (one list for the staff screen)
- "We miss you", "we are waiting for you", "please come back" (คิดถึง, รอคุณอยู่, กลับมาเถอะ). Say: "See you tomorrow?"
- "Why did you stop?", "Where were you?", "What happened?" (ทำไมไม่มา, หายไปไหนมา).
- "Sorry" (ขอโทษ) in any form. For a late reply: "Thank you for your patience with our reply."
- Any count aimed at them: "it has been 9 days", "you only came X times", "4 days a week", "at least". In Thai never "ไม่ต้องมา..."; if days come up the approved line is "ไม่กำหนด 4 วันต่ออาทิตย์ มาฝึกตามเวลาที่สะดวก".
- Countdown and money guilt: "only X days left", "there is still time", "still has some days on it", "you paid for it", "do not waste your pass", "last chance", "hurry".
- Any price, discount, promo, free class, "special for you", "nothing more to pay", "we can work something out".
- "Renew", the next pass, "the choice is yours".
- Health fear or promise: "you will get stiff", "you will lose your progress", "practice will fix it", "you will be fine", any advice on an illness or injury.
- "Many people stop", "it is normal to quit".
- Orders and closes: "Just come", "Say yes and...", "That is all you need to do", "Understood.", "you should", "you must", "do not give up".
- Any answer that argues with their reason ("you can practise before work").
- A chase: a second message with no reply, "just checking in", "did you see my message?".
- Too available: "message me any time, day or night".
- Praise nobody saw: "you were doing so well", "one of our best".
- Words: "pose" (say asana, อาสนะ), "hardcore", "strict", "advanced", "dedicated", "flexible", "old", "lazy", "journey", "unlock", "transform".
- Thai spelling: มายซอร์ not ไมซอร์, สมาชิก not สมาชิค, "เราสอนตั้งแต่เริ่มต้น" not ตั้งแต่ศูนย์, never นะค่ะ or ไหมค่ะ.
- Any quote from Carnegie, Greene, Gracian or a framework. The long dash. More than one question in one message.

## What was changed from the judges' winners
- Message 1: "this week" became "for a few days" (it goes stale after day 9, and short passes flag at 5 days). "No need to explain" was cut from message 1 and message 4: the Thai judge found it hints at blame after one week. It stays once, in the English of the teacher's note.
- "Just come", "Say yes", "That is all you need to do", "there is still time before then" and "the choice is yours" are gone everywhere.
- Message 3 is the booking offer plus the first-time line, with "Your pass is open" in place of "has started".
- Thai follows the Thai judge's rewrites. Two phrases the judges disagreed on were left out: "ครูจะได้รอเจอ" (warm, but close to the banned "รอคุณอยู่") became "จะบอกครูไว้ให้", and both "พักให้เต็มที่" (rest advice) and "ใช้เวลาเท่าที่ต้องการ" (translated) were replaced by "พร้อมเมื่อไหร่ค่อยกลับมาได้เลย".
- Script check on all 13 texts: 0 long dash, 0 digit, 0 banned word, English 20 to 40 words, `{ka}` only after statements.
