## The beats (what each line DOES)
B0 Silent prop: do the "wrong" thing on camera, no words, 5 to 12 s. Then one word that challenges the room's judgment ("What?").
B1 Name the room's belief, then challenge it: "Oh, you all think ___? Let me tell you something."
B2 Lie 1, a number comparison + an everyday object nobody fears: "Do you know ___ is three times ___? Yet if I ___, nobody would say anything."
B3 Lie 2, the false cause: "Do you know the leading cause of ___ is not actually ___? It's ___."
B4 Lie 3, the survivor story: "You could ___ for years and nothing will ever happen to you."
B5 Lie 4, the hidden motive: "This whole war against ___ is just to ___."
B6 Frame break: drop the act, new voice, greet the real audience.
B7 Confession: "I used these arguments, even though I made them up, with ___."
B8 Results with exact small counts, the second one worse: "And the results? ___ of them ___. ___ of them ___."
B9 The moral in one sentence: "___, when said ___ in the right way, can ___."
